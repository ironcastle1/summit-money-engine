## Evidence policy

Primary and official documents are preferred for legal designations, policy decisions, defence announcements and technical notices. Publisher discovery is useful for speed and breadth but high-consequence claims are downgraded when they rely on one domain, anonymous assertions or state-only reporting. Prediction-market prices are retained only as sentiment/context.
