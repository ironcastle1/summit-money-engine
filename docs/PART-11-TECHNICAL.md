# Merlin V20 Part 11 — Conflict Intelligence

Part 11 adds an evidence-graded conflict operating layer over Merlin's existing event registry. It does not create synthetic live events. Empty or unavailable connectors produce empty evidence sets and explicit source states.

## Delivered capabilities

- Conflict-event classification and normalization
- Actor graphs, capabilities, hostile relationships and inferred support links
- Theatre grouping, ranking, conflict phases and confidence
- Frontline grouping and map-ready geometries
- Strike, weapons, force-posture and territorial-change analysis
- Ceasefire agreements, violations and escalation ladders
- Civilian, infrastructure, humanitarian, logistics, economic, energy and food exposure
- Cross-border escalation and regional-contagion analysis
- Contradiction, manipulation and verification-gap analysis
- Scenarios, watchlists, alerts, exports and a dedicated Conflict workspace

## Evidence policy

Measured and independently corroborated records are preferred. Reference, inferred and unavailable inputs remain labelled. Scores are analytical estimates and retain the records that produced them.
