# V10 product direction

The system is redesigned as an information compiler rather than a security roleplay dashboard.

## Customer use case

A user opens the app in the morning and checks:

1. What happened that matters.
2. What can make or save money.
3. What places/routes/markets are affected.
4. What can be turned into a brief, lead list, content post, route check, or small service offer.
5. Which sources actually loaded.

## Value model

The app does not promise guaranteed profit. It supplies opportunity leads and actionable information angles. The user can make the subscription back by turning one useful signal into a small paid output:

- a short intelligence brief
- a lead list
- a client note
- a travel/arrival checklist
- a route review
- a content thread/video/newsletter item
- a market research note
- a simple local-business service pitch

## Main features

- Daily Brief
- Opportunity Radar
- Trend Streams
- Market Impact
- Area Scan
- Route Check
- Source Health
- Map signals
- Save lead workflow

## Removed direction

The product intentionally avoids secret-agent/operator wording. It uses normal professional wording.
