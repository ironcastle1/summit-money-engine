# Merlin V20 Part 09 — Market Intelligence

Part 09 upgrades the existing Markets workspace into an evidence-led market intelligence system. It combines price history, technical state, liquidity, volatility, event exposure, prediction-market signals, source status and transparent opportunity/risk scoring.

## Operating capabilities

- Multi-horizon momentum, trend, volatility, ATR, liquidity and drawdown analysis.
- Breadth, cross-asset correlation, relative strength, heatmaps and regime classification.
- Commodity balance, inventory pressure and supply-demand shock analysis.
- Event-to-market and prediction-market linkage with explanations and relevance scores.
- Opportunity, risk and evidence scoring with visible components and analytical disclosure.
- Saved screens, watchlists, quantified alerts, portfolio exposure and scenario analysis.
- CSV, JSON and summary exports, plus map-ready market-signal features.

## Data integrity policy

Live data is obtained only through configured market, event and prediction connectors. Missing or timed-out connectors produce explicit unavailable states. The platform does not invent live quotes, events or probabilities. Modelled values are identified as analytical estimates and are not investment advice.
