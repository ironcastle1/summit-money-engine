# Merlin V20 Part 08 — Hazard Impact Platform

This package adds the operational hazard-analysis layer. It consumes the existing Merlin event fusion feed and does not invent live observations. Routine earthquakes are rejected unless they cross a major-impact threshold or carry explicit tsunami, infrastructure, sovereign, casualty or shipping impact evidence.

## Delivered capability

- Hazard normalization and classification across twelve operational categories.
- Type-specific impact models for earthquakes, cyclones, floods, wildfires, volcanoes, tsunami, heat, winter weather, drought and landslides.
- Materiality rules, confidence scoring, source freshness, event clustering, tracks and forecast windows.
- Population, infrastructure, logistics, economic and casualty scenario exposure.
- Cascading-risk analysis and network disruption propagation.
- Portfolio exposure, alert thresholds, geofenced watchlists, timelines and exports.
- Map-only hazard panel with live list, detail inspection, scenarios and watches.

All exposure and loss outputs are labelled modelled estimates. They are not represented as observed casualties, confirmed damage or authoritative forecasts.
