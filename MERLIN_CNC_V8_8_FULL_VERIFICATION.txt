V8.8 user-facing fixes
1. Image -> DXF no longer stops after one threshold attempt. It tries multiple deterministic subject-isolation passes and chooses the strongest plausible connected result. If all passes fail, it asks for a closer crop or explicit Dark/Light foreground instead of creating an empty DXF.
2. Image-derived DXFs are emitted as AutoCAD R12 ASCII POLYLINE/VERTEX geometry for Fusion compatibility.
3. The DXF Resizer fixing-hole selector now defaults to No fixing holes, with 2 top holes and 4 corner-region holes as optional choices.
